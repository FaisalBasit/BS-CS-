WRITING CV / RESUME
 What Is a resume?
A resume is a short, concise docum- ent used for job
applications. The purpose of a resume is to provide
recruiters with a brief overview of the candidate’s work
history. A good resume should be targeted at a specific job
and be 1–2 pages long.
