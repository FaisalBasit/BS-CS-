Human Resource Management (HRM)
It  is the management of employees of a company  in  such  a  way 
that employees performance is maximized according to company’s 
strategic objectives.
 Strategic objectives  means  aims  and  objectives of the company.
 Like  many  other  fields, HRM  is  very  important  for the software 
development work in an organization. 
Responsibility of HRM
 An organization  has expectations  from  employees, hence  the employees should be able to manage themselves and their work.
 HRM  is the responsibility of management.
The management should make sure the maximum utilization ofhuman resources available to the organization.
Management
A group of people who take decisions about an organization.
Management Levels
- Strategic (Top Level) Management 
- Tactical (Middle Level) Management
- Operational (Front-line) Management
Strategic Management
Includes 
 - Chairman/Managing Director (MD)/Chief  
- Executive Officer (CEO)
- Board of Directors (BOD)
Develop organizational goals and strategic objectives
E.g. starting a new branch of company or introducing a new product 
in the market
