the software and paid taxes to the Canadian Tax department. The
Tax Department sent notices to the clients and penalized for
submitting wrong statements.
Ethical decision making: Case 3
 Q.1 Which type of code of ethics applied here?
 Q.2 What were the mistakes of software house?
A.1 The software house did  not honor contract,  agreements, and 
assigned responsibilities. (ACM Code)

the software before handing over to the clients. 
(ii) The  software  house  had false  contracts  with  the clients.
