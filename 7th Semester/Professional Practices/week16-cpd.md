Continuing Professional Development cycle
Continuing professional development (CPD) is the ongoing
process of identifying professional goals, developing a plan
to achieve them, and documenting the experience.
Stages include:-
Stage1 :- Identify needs.
Stage2 :- Planning.
Stage3 :- Action or doing.
Stage4 :- Reflecting on learning.
Stage5 :- Implement your new learning. 
Stages of CPD cycle
Stage1 :- Identifying your needs, means what is your goal.
pushing you potential in your career involves strategic thinking
Take a moment to summarize your current career path and
Imagine great milestones for the future. Now, construct a road-
Map. A roadmap of achievable small goals that you can actua-
ly achieve your bigger goals.
Stage2 :- Planning means set goals and make a plan. Identify
gaps, areas of growth and opportunities to help you progress
to the next stage in your career. SMART goals based on these
needs, outlining activities and resources required to achieve
your objectives.
Stages of CPD cycle (continues)
Stage3  :- Action means start planning your learning activities  
Plan to attend for training or coaching sessions, enroll in certi-
ficate courses,   attend   webinars,  conferences,  and   learn 
courses from udemy /  courseera / udacity / the great learning / 
google free courses.
Stage4 :- Reflection on your experiences. Consider  what you 
have  learned and  how it   has  influenced  your work.  Writing 
down these observations helps you reflect on your progression 
and evolve your approach. Identify any personal and  professi-
onal development needs or areas for further improvement. 
Stages of CPD cycle (continues)
Stage5 :- Implementation stage offers regularly promote intern-
al training with links to self-directed learning and communicate
the process for requesting online courses and one should not
stop after completing one or two certificate course, he / she
Should continuously participate in life long learning activities.
Example for CPD cycle using SMART Goal
.
SMART goal example for learning English :-
Specific:- I want to learn how to have conversations in English to have better
communication with my clients while doing free lancing.
Measurable :- I will use a daily language learning App on cell to hold me
accountable and track my progress.
Attainable:- I know English speakers on my different social media accounts to
whom i can practice English with them.
Relevant:- I am a free lancer and most of my clients are native English
speakers so its mandatory and relevant for me to learn English.
Time-bound:- I want to learn English in next 3 months so i can at least
communicate to my clients and complete online projects on time.
