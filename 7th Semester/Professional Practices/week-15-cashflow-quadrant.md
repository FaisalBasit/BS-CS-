Cash flow Quadrant Explained
E - Employee
 E quadrant belongs to employee who has a job. This is where most
people earn their income. Employee can make little or lot of money, but
when employee stops working, or when the business stops, their income
stops too.
 An Employee financial destiny, security and freedom is based upon the
success of their employer.
 Low Level – Middle Level – Top Level.
S – Self Employed
The S quadrant includes those people who prefer to work for themselves and prefer
to be their own boss.
Include dentist, lawyers, restaurant owners, insurance agents, property agents, sales
person etc..
Many self employed people generate large income and obviously they do have lot of
control than an employee but when they stop working, their income also stops.
Means in the long run lead to tiredness and fatigue.
B – Business Owner
The B quadrant own a system and lead or supervise people, system
automatically runs and owner of system or business earns money with out
constant involvement.
Sometimes same type of business could be run by S owners and B
owners.
 Example:- If a person plead a case in court belongs to S quadrant and if
he owns a law firm and junior counsel plead belong to B quadrant.
I – Investors
I quadrant belongs to people who own assets that produce income, investors in
this quadrant have usually accumulated money earned from one or more of the
other three quadrants.
Also called passive income. This obviously lead to greater growth in less hard
work and obviously have more financial freedom.
Those people
Example:- share holders, business traders, property investors, social media
influencer.
