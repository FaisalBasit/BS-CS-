The Benefit Cost Ratio Method 
 Benefit Cost Ratio B/C or BCR or also called Benefit Cost Analysis (BCA). It 
is  a  technique  that  is  widely  used  to  assess  or  evaluate  an  alternate  or  a 
project to find out whether benefits are higher than costs or vice - versa. B/C or BCR or BCA is method to find out PV, annual cost and ROR. B/C provides a means for comparing benefits to costs.   The  objective  of  Chapter  on  Benefit  Cost  Ratio  is  how  to  compare  2 
alternatives on the basis of benefit/cost ratio. This method is required as a 
technique.  The higher the benefits the better  B/C = AWB/AWC  B/C > 1  Project desirable.  Methods of determining B/C  1.  Conventional Method  2.  Modified Method   
Conventional Method  
 Conventional Method is older than Modified Method  In conventional method there is it is not possible to calculate Dis-benefit 
(DB)  As a first step calculate value of CR and then find value of B/C ratio   B/C = B / CR + O&M --------------------------   (1)      CR = (I – F) [i(1+i)N / ( 1+ i)N-1] + F(i) ------ (2)                             Where  B = Annual Worth Benefit {AWB) CR = Capital Recovery Cost – Initial investment converted to annual cost. O&M = Operation & Maintenance Expenses of proposed project F = Future Worth or Salvage Value (SV) B/C > 1  Project Desirable.
