# Business Plan and Investor Presentation Notes

## Topic
**FYP: AI Automation Solutions for Zero Lifestyle**

This FYP combines two products for **Zero Lifestyle**:

1. **Selectra-HR** - AI recruitment automation
2. **AI Customer Support Calling Agent** - bilingual voice support automation

For exams and presentation, explain both as one business:

**Zero Lifestyle AI Automation Suite**

This makes the idea simple:

> We are building AI tools that help Zero Lifestyle hire better and handle customer support faster.

---

## 1. Quick Understanding

### What is a Business Plan?
A business plan is a detailed document that explains:

- what the business does
- who the customers are
- how it will operate
- how it will earn money
- how it will grow

### What is an Investor Presentation?
An investor presentation is a short slide summary of the business plan.  
Its purpose is to convince investors, teachers, judges, or partners.

### Difference
- **Business plan** = detailed explanation
- **Investor presentation** = short and convincing summary

### Why this FYP is important
- It solves real business problems.
- It is built for a real company, Zero Lifestyle.
- It shows technical plus business value.

---

## 2. Main FYP Idea

Our FYP is one business with two solutions:

- **Selectra-HR** for recruitment automation
- **AI Calling Agent** for customer support automation

These two systems help Zero Lifestyle:

- hire faster

- improve candidate screening
- answer customer calls in Urdu and English

> An AI automation business that improves hiring and customer support for growing companies, starting with Zero Lifestyle.

## 3.3 Market Analysis

### Market need
Companies spend too much time on:

- recruitment
- customer support

These are repetitive tasks, so they are suitable for AI automation.

### Problem 1: Recruitment
Many companies face:

- too many CVs
- slow screening
- unfair or inconsistent evaluation
- difficulty in shortlisting candidates

### Problem 2: Customer Support
Many companies face:

- repeated questions from customers
- high call volume
- inconsistent answers
- high cost of human support teams

### Target market
- Zero Lifestyle
- Pakistani ecommerce businesses
- retail and lifestyle brands
- SMEs with regular hiring and support needs

### Why the opportunity is strong
- businesses want to save time and cost
- automation demand is growing
- Urdu voice support is still limited in the market

### Competitive advantage
Our solution is better because it offers:

- practical workflow automation
- AI scoring and shortlisting
- bilingual voice support
- a real business use case
